01 / THE UNITY EXPERIENCE

One TV screen. Every kind of entertainment.

Unity is Signal’s foundation: Live TV is the home you can always return to, while movies, shows, recommendations, previews and media details live alongside it. Explore a film profile without losing the context behind it, move between the channel grid and cinematic catalogs, and return naturally to the television experience that started it all. Unity is a shared environment, not a stack of isolated destinations.

One connected surface • Context preserved • Shared navigation

02 / LIVE TELEVISION

Your channels. A living TV grid.

Live TV is the home of Unity. Browse a visual 4 × 3 channel grid with program artwork, channel names and Now & Next information. Move through pages, open your categories or guide, and choose a channel from the same television environment that connects the rest of Signal.

Visual channel grid • Program artwork • Now & Next • Channel pages

Preview a channel before you open it.

Focus a channel to see its live preview inside the grid. The current program, channel identity and upcoming information stay beside the picture, so you can explore what is on while keeping the rest of your channels in view.

Find the channels you want.

Open the category panel over the grid to move between All Channels, favorites and provider groups such as HD, Sports, Drama or News. Your channel view remains in the background as you narrow the selection.

See the schedule at a glance.

The electronic program guide brings channels, schedules and a live preview together. Scan upcoming programs and switch between channel groups from one view, with the selected program’s details close at hand.

The full guide, with more room.

Collapse the category rail to give the full program timetable more space. Current-program details and a live preview stay above the schedule, while episode alerts can appear in the same view. The guide adapts around what you want to explore without taking you out of Unity.

Entertainment isn't a collection of apps. It's one experience.

We built Signal around a simple conviction: television shouldn't be fragmented. A channel you're watching, a film you're curious about, a trailer, a cast member, and the next episode should feel like parts of the same story. BUILT FROM AN IDEA · REFINED THROUGH REAL-WORLD TESTING · DESIGNED FOR THE LONG TERM
“

FROM THE CREATOR

Signal began with a conviction: entertainment should not be divided into separate apps, disconnected screens and isolated features. I envisioned Unity as a single living television space, where Live TV, cinematic discovery, cast and episode information, streaming sources, artwork, previews and playback interact as parts of the same experience.

Before building this vision into Signal, I personally reached out to developers and shared the concept of a unified entertainment experience. Within days of those conversations, I saw other apps begin presenting ideas that looked strikingly similar. The timing did not feel random to me. I felt that an idea I had shared in good faith had been taken, and that experience was deeply upsetting.

I cannot independently establish what influenced another developer or prove that any specific app copied my work. But I can speak clearly about what I experienced, why the sequence of events raised serious concerns for me, and why I believe creators deserve honesty, acknowledgment and respect when they share original ideas.

What I felt was missing from those other approaches is still the heart of Signal: Unity is not merely multiple services under one roof. It is one connected environment, where each layer understands and complements the others. The goal is not another menu of separate destinations; it is television that feels continuous, personal and alive.

Turning that vision into an actual Apple TV app has taken sustained development, difficult technical decisions, repeated testing, setbacks and countless refinements. I am committed to supporting and evolving Signal for the long term. This project is my way of standing behind the idea I shared—and building the complete experience I always imagined.

— The creator of Signal

FREQUENTLY ASKED

The details matter.

What makes Signal different?+

Signal is built around Unity: live channels, on-demand catalogs, media profiles and playback share a consistent interface. You can move between them without managing separate apps.

Does Signal include movies or television channels?+

No. Signal is a discovery and playback interface. You connect your own compatible sources and services; access to content depends on those services and your rights to use them.

What is AetherEngine?+

AetherEngine is the playback technology used by Signal across its Live TV, on-demand and preview experiences. Specific playback capabilities can depend on format, device and app version.

Can I customize the interface?+

Signal offers multiple browsing approaches and visual preferences, including themes, catalog styles and player options. Exact controls depend on the installed build.

How do notifications work?+

Signal includes episode-alert and notification-related features. Availability, reliability and permission behavior depend on the build and device settings.

Are trailers and previews supported?+

Signal supports live channel previews, previews inside selected catalog cards, and floating trailers opened from media profiles. Preview availability depends on the title, source and installed build; these experiences continue to be refined as development progresses.

Can I connect debrid services and Trakt?+

Signal supports compatible debrid integrations for source resolution and Trakt-connected discovery or watchlist workflows. You supply your own accounts; Signal does not provide subscriptions to those services.

Where can I install Signal?+

The Apple TV/tvOS experience is under active development. Public distribution details will be provided when they are confirmed.

How do Trakt and Resume work together?+

With your connected Trakt account, Signal can bring watchlists and recommendations into discovery while its Resume experience helps you pick up movies and episodes. Sync behavior and removal controls vary by build.

What do VOD cache and Live TV timeshift do?+

Live TV timeshift lets you move within a buffered window when the channel and stream support it. VOD cache stores temporary playback data to help manage seeking and playback. Cache capacity and indicators vary by app version.

What is MediaFlow Proxy integration?+

Signal offers regular MediaFlow Proxy and MediaFlow Proxy Light configuration at the application level. Supported playback requests can use the configured route without setting a separate proxy in every add-on; actual coverage depends on the integration and source.

Can I install through Signulous?+

Signulous has been used to install Signal tvOS builds during testing. Availability, signing, updates and device requirements depend on the distribution method and current build; there is no implied official App Store release.

Will Signal support the next Apple TV?+

Signal is designed for tvOS and is being developed with future Apple TV hardware in mind. Compatibility and performance on a new device can only be confirmed once that hardware and its tvOS version are available for testing.
