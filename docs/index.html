THE FEATURES IN DETAIL

Built around the way you watch.

LIVE TELEVISION

Channels, guides and live previews.

Connect Channels DVR, M3U or Xtream Codes sources. Browse the visual channel grid, categories, Now & Next information, the full program guide and Quick Guide, with live previews to help you choose. Source support and program data depend on your provider.

TITLE DISCOVERY

Explore a title in place.

TMDB-powered metadata and artwork bring context to movies and series. Open glass media profiles with cast portraits, the cast wall, episode cards, ratings, trailer actions and viewing options while the surrounding catalog stays in view. These overlays continue to evolve.

AETHERENGINE

Playback, with room to move.

AetherEngine powers movies, episodes, Live TV and previews. Use seek controls, audio and subtitle selection, temporary VOD caching, and Live TV timeshift where supported. Available controls and buffered playback depend on the stream and installed build.

SOURCE CONNECTIONS

Configure your services once.

Connect your own compatible debrid accounts and add-ons for on-demand source resolution. Regular MediaFlow Proxy and MediaFlow Proxy Light can be configured at app level for supported playback paths. Routing depends on the source and integration.

YOUR WATCH JOURNEY

Keep your progress close.

Bring connected Trakt watchlists and recommendations into discovery, save favorites, and return to movies and episodes through Resume. Viewing-history sync and removal controls depend on the installed build.

EPISODE ALERTS

Know what is coming next.

Episode-related alerts and notification options help keep upcoming viewing in reach. Availability and behavior depend on device settings and the Signal version you have installed.

BUILT FROM AN IDEA · REFINED THROUGH REAL-WORLD TESTING · DESIGNED FOR THE LONG TERM
“

FROM THE CREATOR

Signal began with a conviction: entertainment should not be divided into separate apps, disconnected screens and isolated features. I envisioned Unity as a single living television space, where Live TV, cinematic discovery, cast and episode information, streaming sources, artwork, previews and playback interact as parts of the same experience.

Before building this vision into Signal, I personally reached out to developers and shared the concept of a unified entertainment experience. Within days of those conversations, I saw other apps begin presenting ideas that looked strikingly similar. The timing did not feel random to me. I felt that an idea I had shared in good faith had been taken, and that experience was deeply upsetting.

I cannot independently establish what influenced another developer or prove that any specific app copied my work. But I can speak clearly about what I experienced, why the sequence of events raised serious concerns for me, and why I believe creators deserve honesty, acknowledgment and respect when they share original ideas.

What I felt was missing from those other approaches is still the heart of Signal: Unity is not merely multiple services under one roof. It is one connected environment, where each layer understands and complements the others. The goal is not another menu of separate destinations; it is television that feels continuous, personal and alive.

Turning that vision into an actual Apple TV app has taken sustained development, difficult technical decisions, repeated testing, setbacks and countless refinements. I am committed to supporting and evolving Signal for the long term. This project is my way of standing behind the idea I shared—and building the complete experience I always imagined.

— The creator of Signal

FREQUENTLY ASKED

The details matter.

Does Signal include movies or television channels?+

No. Signal is a discovery and playback interface. You connect your own compatible sources and services; access to content depends on those services and your rights to use them.

How can I install Signal?+

See Availability and membership for the current distribution information and release details.

Do I need separate service accounts?+

Only for the integrations you choose to use. You supply your own compatible provider, debrid or Trakt accounts; Signal does not include subscriptions to those services.

Why can features vary between setups?+

Stream format, provider support, device settings, tvOS version and the installed Signal build can affect the available controls. Features marked as planned or in development are still being refined.

Will Signal support future Apple TV hardware?+

Signal is being developed with future Apple TV hardware in mind. Compatibility and performance can be confirmed once the device and its tvOS version are available for testing.
